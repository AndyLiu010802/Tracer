Transparent plant atlases were visually inspected. Architecture and terrain atlases are opaque hand-painted material swatches applied to actual three-dimensional geometry. Plants use closed textured relief meshes with generated front/back surfaces and silhouette walls, rather than camera-facing sprites. This is a hybrid illustration/3D treatment, not a fully sculpted botanical model.
