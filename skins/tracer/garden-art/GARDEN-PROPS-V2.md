Generated with the built-in imagegen tool using `spring-garden-v1.png` as a style and camera reference. Original generated PNG alpha is preserved, with no raster post-processing.

- `garden-bench-v2.png`: 1402 x 1122, transparent PNG.
- `garden-lantern-v2.png`: 1024 x 1536, transparent PNG, reused for both path lamps.
- Integration: `../garden-world-view.js`, rendered with `image-rendering: pixelated` and the existing level unlock classes.

- Visual checks: `dev/qa-garden-props.cjs` verifies alpha, decoding, level visibility and desktop/mobile framing.

## Bench Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one production game sprite, NOT a scene edit. The input image is a STYLE AND CAMERA REFERENCE ONLY. Subject: a small lovingly weathered wooden garden bench matching the pixel-art cottage garden reference. Genuine transparent PNG background with alpha, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no grass tile, no landscape, no text. Three-quarter isometric view from above, long axis runs from lower-left to upper-right at about 25 degrees, backrest on the upper-left/rear side, front seat edge on lower-right. Four honey-oak narrow seat slats with visible gaps, two backrest slats, solid timber legs, end supports, small iron bolts. Believable joinery and perspective, chunky stepped pixel edges, carefully shaded wood grain using small clustered pixels, darkest warm brown under the seat, subtle cool green reflected light on legs, warm sunshine from upper-left, no smooth vector surfaces, no blurry/painterly edges. Match the detail density of the fence and cottage in the reference: cozy polished pixel-art game prop, not photorealistic, not cartoon icon, not plastic, no exaggerated chunky outline. The whole bench must be visible and fill about 85 percent of a square transparent canvas, centered, with a restrained translucent pixel-shaped contact shadow beneath feet. Only one bench, nothing else. Aim for apparent native sprite resolution about 100 by 80 pixels, rendered crisp with nearest-neighbor enlarged pixel clusters.

## Lantern Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate ONE production transparent pixel-art game sprite, not a scene edit. Input image is only a STYLE, LIGHTING AND CAMERA reference. Subject: a small rustic garden lantern on a short wooden post, the cottage-path furnishing that belongs in the reference's sunny garden. Genuine transparent PNG alpha background, absolutely no painted checkerboard, no grass tile or background. Entire object visible centered on a portrait canvas, tight composition with around 10 percent transparent margin. Three-quarter isometric view from above at the same camera angle as cottage/fence. A short sturdy weathered honey-oak post, attached small square lantern with four clearly visible dark bronze corner bars, a peaked copper cap with layered rim, translucent amber glass panels and a small cream-gold lamp inside. Must read as an actual three-dimensional garden lantern, NOT as a flat sign or icon: distinguish the front and side glass planes, roof thickness, little finial, post grain and foot. Restrained soft amber light in daylight, no glow halo. Sunlit upper-left edges, shadowed right side, small subtle translucent contact shadow under post. Crisp clustered square pixels and stepped edges with warm wood tones, cool reflected green and dark brown shadows matching reference cottage door lantern and fence, no smooth vector fills, no photorealism, no blurry illustration. Intended displayed native scale about 22 pixels wide by 58 pixels high, so silhouette and few lovely material details matter more than tiny detail. Nearest-neighbor pixel art upscale; one lantern only, no text or other objects.
