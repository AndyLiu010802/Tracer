# Planet meadow material v1

Generated with the built-in image generation tool (stylized-concept). Used as an overhead ground material on the live 3D garden, not as a static planet picture. Receipts, flowers, surface lighting, rotation, terrain depth, and hit testing remain rendered by the application.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square, opaque TOP-DOWN albedo game terrain texture for a rotatable miniature pastoral garden planet. Create a beautifully hand-painted, highly detailed cozy cottage-garden GROUND SURFACE viewed perfectly orthographically from directly overhead, no perspective, no globe, no framing, no sky. All pixels must be usable terrain. Quiet sage and moss-green meadow turf across the full square, tiny layered grass tufts, clover carpets, muted yellow-green moss, softly worn earth, scattered minute pebbles. High-end illustrated cozy farming game aesthetic with delicate painterly detail and soft diffuse illumination, no baked directional cast shadows. Coordinate layout important for mapping to a sphere: a small organic kidney-shaped turquoise pond centered at 32% of image width, 55% of image height, occupying 28% image width and 38% image height. Pond clear teal water with intricate faint ripples, a few tiny lily leaves and soft naturally irregular mossy pebble shoreline. A narrow worn cream-sand meandering path, about 5% image width, running vertically from top edge near x=53%, curving through x=55% at image middle and to x=57% at bottom edge; stay to the RIGHT of pond. Natural unplanted meadow on all other ground areas, with irregular fern-green patches that will receive separate flower sprites at runtime. Rich microdetail but calm broad values, inviting and precious miniature garden. No flowers or fruit, no trees, no buildings, no faces, no animals, no objects projecting up from the surface, no text, no borders, no checkerboard, no transparency. This is a flat terrain material, NOT a scene preview or a 3D rendered sphere. 1024x1024 or larger square.
